      About Liimy Land

      Liimy Land is a college showcase project: a cute miniature hawker market for children, drawn in a bright cartoon style inspired by Shinchan.

      Two rows of little G+2 buildings face each other. Hawker carts line the middle of the road, with walkways on both sides. The sun is setting pink and orange, and gold lights are switching on.

      Visit on a computer, a phone, or a Meta Quest headset in VR.
