How to explore

Looking around and moving are separate. Drag or turn your head to look. Use the four arrows to travel to a connected viewpoint.

↑←→↓
LookDrag with mouse or finger. In VR, turn your head.
MoveW A S D, arrow keys, the on-screen arrows, or flick a Quest thumbstick.
SelectClick, tap, or pull the Quest trigger while pointing at a button.
ReturnThe centre button steps back through where you have been.

Connect your Meta Quest

The app runs in the headset's browser. There is nothing to pair; you only open this page on the Quest.

  1. Put on your Quest and open Meta Quest Browser.
  2. Open this page's address (copy it, then send it to the headset with the Meta Horizon phone app, or type it in).
  3. Select Check headset below. It should report that VR is supported.
  4. On a PC, you can also use Quest Link and open the page in Chrome or Edge.

VR needs a secure (https) address. Hosted pages already have one.
