How to explore ✨

Looking around and moving are separate. Drag or turn your head to look. Use the four arrows to travel to a connected viewpoint.

↑←→↓
Look 👀Drag with mouse or finger. In VR, turn your head.
Move 🐾Click the floor arrows like Street View, or use W A S D, the buttons, or a Quest thumbstick.
Select 💖Click, tap, or pull the Quest trigger while pointing at a button.
Return 🐰The centre button steps back through where you have been.

Upload your 360° images 🌈

Add Enscape panoramas (2:1 JPG, PNG or WebP). Place them on the map, link them with arrows, adjust the look, then view them in VR.

Upload ☁️Drop one or many images. They stay on this device.
Map and arrows 🗺️Drag pins on the map corner, then choose which scene each arrow leads to.
Style 🎨Brightness, contrast, colour, warmth and direction per image.
Resolution 🔍Settings lets you cap image size so big renders run smoothly on Quest.
