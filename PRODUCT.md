# Product context: Liimy Land

_Written from the owner's brief (Telugu/English chat), no separate interview._

## What it is
Liimy Land is a college showcase project: a cute miniature hawker market for children. It has a Shinchan-inspired cartoon look; the look only, with no characters or logos.
- **Layout:** G+2 buildings on both sides of a street, hawker carts down the middle of the road, and a pedestrian walkway on each side of the carts.
- **Mood:** pink-orange sunset with gold lights.

## Who it's for
- Children, and the college reviewers watching the showcase.
- Visitors on desktop, phone and **Meta Quest** (VR).

## Primary jobs
1. **Look around:** a 360° viewer with seven spots, arrows and a mini map.
2. **Interact along:** walk around and pick things up from the carts (fruit, vegetables, flowers, pottery, toys and more). Activities:
   - pick a basket
   - get an ice cream from the hawker
   - pick flowers
   - bag a vegetable and a fruit

## Flow
"Liimy Land, Let's go" → a small About popup with ✕ → mode picker (Look around / Interact along, each also available in VR).

## Pinned constraints
- Not traditional. Cute and toy-like.
- Normal push carts, like the reference photo of a street snack cart.
- Static site, local assets only. Music is supplied by the owner and starts only after entering a mode.
- Publishing is a separate, explicitly approved step.
